\## 2. Objective



Taskora aims to provide students with a centralized platform to organize academic tasks, plan study activities, manage notes, monitor deadlines, and track productivity.



\## 3. Key Features



\* \*\*Dashboard:\*\* Overview of student activities and productivity.

\* \*\*Task Management:\*\* Organize and manage academic tasks.

\* \*\*Planner:\*\* Plan and structure study activities.

\* \*\*Focus Workspace:\*\* Support focused study sessions.

\* \*\*Notes:\*\* Organize study-related notes.

\* \*\*Analytics:\*\* View productivity and task progress.

\* \*\*Authentication:\*\* Login, registration, and password recovery.

\* \*\*Profile Management:\*\* Access profile-related information.
